Questions, answered

Are the cars new or pre-owned?

Both. Every pre-owned car passes our 1,200-point inspection and carries a two-year warranty.

Can I test drive before buying?

Yes. Book a private visit and your chosen car will be ready at the time you pick.

Do you deliver outside Delhi NCR?

We deliver across India in enclosed carriers with insurance during transit.

Can I trade in my current car?

Yes. We value it on the spot and adjust it against the new price.
